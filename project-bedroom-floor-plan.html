Bedroom Layout Study

Spatial planning for a 14 m² bedroom — balancing function and calm

Every square metre counts when a room needs to hold a full life. This sketch grew out of a real constraint: a bedroom serving as sleeping space, reading corner, and dressing area without feeling cramped. The wardrobe anchors the entrance wall to keep the sleeping zone visually clear, the reading chair sits at the window for natural light, and an asymmetric nightstand arrangement widens the main passage without touching overall footprint.

Massimiliano Berardi · 2026
